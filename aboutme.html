About Me

My name is Moses Ogwang. I am a Police Officer serving in the Uganda Police Force since 2014, with a specialization in security operations, incident response, and ICT-related tasks. Over the years, I have built a career that combines discipline in law enforcement with innovation in technology. This unique blend has allowed me to contribute both to public safety and to the digital transformation of institutions.

Personal Background

I was born and raised in Uganda, where I developed a strong sense of responsibility and service to community. My journey has been shaped by resilience, adaptability, and a passion for learning. These values have guided me through my education, professional life, and personal growth.

Education

I hold a Diploma in Information Technology (Distinction) from Apex International University. My academic journey also includes the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) from Soroti Senior Secondary School and the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) from Serere Secondary School. At Apex, I excelled in system administration, web development (PHP, HTML, CSS, MySQL, Python), and computer networks. My dedication to excellence earned me a Certificate of Appreciation for emerging best in course.

Professional Experience

In my role as a Police Officer, I have handled ICT support, digital system deployment, and incident response. I am currently attached to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, where I have served for the past six years. This attachment has broadened my perspective, allowing me to contribute to national ICT development and institutional collaboration.
